5. Enterprise Compliance & Security Highlights

🔒 Multi-Tenant RLS Architecture

PostgreSQL Row Level Security ensures tenant data is completely isolated. Organization A cannot access Organization B records.

🛡️ Zero PII Exposure (Privacy Guard)

Regular team members cannot see email addresses or phone numbers. Only public display names and usernames are exposed.

📜 Tamper-Proof Audit Logging

All administrative oversight actions append timestamped records into the audit database, preventing unauthorized snooping.

📱 Full Mobile & Desktop Support

Adaptive responsive UI: WhatsApp Web on desktop/tablets and WhatsApp Mobile App single-view navigation on phones.
